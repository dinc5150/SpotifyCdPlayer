1. Your Wi-Fi

Pick your home network. For a hidden network, type its name.

    2. This device

    .local

    Where to find this page later. Give each device its own if you have more than one.

    Protects Wi-Fi, settings and updates from others on your network. Username: admin.

    3. Spotify app

    The device talks to Spotify through your own free developer app. At developer.spotify.com/dashboard, create an app with the Web API, add the redirect URI below, and paste its Client ID here. You can also do this later under Manage.

    Redirect URI to add in the Spotify app: https://<you>.github.io/<repo>/
